Build target

No menus needed — you know these already.

Car stats — one screen

All on the Upgrades stat panel (toggle the detailed display) after your last part is bought — read it once, top to bottom.
PI is the big number on the same panel. Enter it and the Build Plan works out how much budget you have left to the class ceiling.

What's fitted — defaults follow class & event

These gate what the game lets you tune, so anything not fitted comes back blank rather than as a number you cannot enter. Compound and width show on the Tires tab; gear count is the number in the transmission's name.
Optional — skip unless you already know these
Size from the tire upgrade screen; redline reads off the right edge of the Gearing-tab graph; top speed from the stats screen. All three together solve final drive exactly — but you dial final drive on the in-game speed graph anyway, so skipping these is fine.
